### What I Am Building

I work at the intersection of molecular biology, sequencing data, and machine learning. My current research focuses on context-aware regulatory modeling: not just asking whether a motif exists, but whether the cellular context makes binding, accessibility, or regulation biologically plausible.

```text
DNA sequence + RNA context + chromatin accessibility + causal networks + protein structure
                              -> regulatory prediction
```

- Developing context-aware transcription factor binding models using ChIP-seq, ATAC-seq, RNA-seq, methylome features, AlphaFold structures, and causal regulatory networks.
- Applying CLIP-seq analysis and deep-learning approaches for RNA-binding protein binding-site prediction.
- Building reproducible high-throughput sequencing workflows for QC, alignment, normalization, peak calling, and downstream biological interpretation.
- Moving comfortably between wet-lab biology, Linux pipelines, statistical modeling, and neural network design.
